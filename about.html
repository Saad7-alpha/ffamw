Welcome to Noor Forces Academy

Noor Forces Academy is Pakistan's premier institution dedicated to preparing young men and women for initial tests of Pakistan Armed Forces. With over a decade of excellence, we have established ourselves as the trusted choice for aspiring candidates.

Our comprehensive approach combines academic rigor, physical training, and psychological preparation to ensure our students are fully equipped to succeed in the competitive selection process.
